HOW THE ENTRY SUGGESTION WORKS

Example: choose OVER, barrier 3 and trigger digit 0. The scanner displays an entry alert only when the latest tick digit equals 0 and the last 100-digit sample shows at least 60% outcomes above the selected barrier.

For OVER 3, winning final digits are 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9. The trigger digit (0) is the digit you wait to see before considering entry. It is not the contract's winning digit.
